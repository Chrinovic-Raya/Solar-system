## Calculations (5% of FISA)

### The calculations has mostly to do with calculating the values of resistors and capacitors. Some resistor and capacitor values are given. Thus, you have to use these resistor and capacitor values to calculate the other resistor and capacitor values. Do not calculate resistor and capacitor values that were provided to you.

Photo of Simulated Circuit (10% of FISA)

Take a screenshot of the hardware simulation part of Tinkercad. Paste the screenshot into this position of the documentation.

Photo of Physical Circuit (10% of FISA)

You are required to take a photo of the physical layout of your “solar charge controller”. Make sure all the connections to all the components are clearly visible. Make sure your student card is visible in the picture as well. This picture must be added to this position in your documentation.

Program code (35% of FISA)

Copy and paste the code as text into this part. Do not insert pictures or screenshots of code. The program should be able to do the following:

* Charge the capacitors as far as possible.
* Switch capacitors from series to parallel and back to minimise loses while providing appropriate voltages to the load.
* Adjust output voltage using infrared remote. The output voltage must be sensed and this sensed voltage must be used to adjust the output voltage to be the required voltage.
* Show voltages on LCD display. These voltages should be calibrated. When the output voltage is changed by the user then the LCD screen should provide a display that will help the user insert a new value.
* Create custom characters to show when capacitors are in series and when in parallel.
